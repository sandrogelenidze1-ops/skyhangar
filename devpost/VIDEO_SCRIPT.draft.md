# SkyHangar: video script (target 2:45, hard limit 3:00)

Record the Lens Studio preview, then record the voiceover separately and lay it over the footage. Don't use copyrighted music. Quiet ambient sound is fine. Add captions for the voiceover lines.
Items marked [CHECK] depend on what the build actually delivers. Cut or reword anything that isn't working on camera.

## 0:00 to 0:12: Hook
Screen: slow look-up shot of the virtual sky. Small markers drift, with no labels yet.
VO: "Right now there are planes above you. Hundreds of them. And you don't know a single one."

## 0:12 to 0:25: The idea
Screen: title card "SkyHangar", then back to the sky.
VO: "SkyHangar is a Lens for Specs that turns the sky into something you can reach. Look up. Tap. Pull it down. Keep it."

## 0:25 to 0:55: Look up
Screen: look around. Planes, helicopters, satellites in a different color. Aim at one plane: trail appears, solid behind it and dotted ahead, with origin and destination codes. Turn away from all aircraft: the edge arrow pulses toward the nearest one, with its airline label.
VO: "Every plane carries its story across the sky. Where it took off, where it's landing. Satellites are up there too, in their own color. And if you look the wrong way, an arrow points you home."

## 0:55 to 1:20: Tap for the story
Screen: quick pinch on a plane. The card drops down: airline, flight number, route, takeoff and landing times, aircraft type, altitude, speed.
VO: "Tap a plane and it tells you everything. Who's flying it, where it's headed, how high, how fast."

## 1:20 to 1:55: Pull it down
Screen: pinch, hold and drag. The plane slides down the sky into the hand, as a small 3D model in the airline's colors. The glowing pad fades in. Release over it: "Collected".
VO: "Now pinch it. And pull. The plane leaves the sky and lands in your hand. Drop it on the pad, and it's yours."

## 1:55 to 2:20: Your hangar
Screen: open the Hangar from the bar. The shelf with plaques (airline, flight, route). Pick one up and throw it: it glides away and returns. [CHECK] The helicopter spins its rotors and returns.
VO: "Every plane you catch goes in your hangar, with the airline and route on its plaque. Throw one like a paper plane. It always comes back."

## 2:20 to 2:35: Radar
Screen: tap Radar on the bar. The tilted disc with sweep line and blips. Point and pinch a blip to open its card.
VO: "Can't look up? Switch to radar. The whole sky from above, one pinch away."

## 2:35 to 2:50: Built with CLAD
Screen: quick cuts. The design brief, Lens Studio with the Lens Studio MCP connected, the LEAF test results panel. Then back to the sky.
VO: "I'm a designer, not a coder. I planned this with plan-lens, built it with CLAD through the Lens Studio MCP, and tested it with LEAF. The demo runs on a sample sky of recorded flight data."
Close, on screen: "SkyHangar. Look up. Pull the sky into your hands."

## Voiceover notes
- About 250 words in total. Speak slowly, with a pause after each scene.
- Say "Specs" the way Snap does and keep each line short.
- If a feature isn't working by Nov 7, cut its scene and shorten the video. Never show something that doesn't work.
